# User Role Journeys

This document outlines the main journey for each user role in the platform, from onboarding to everyday use.

## 1. Student Journey

### Goal
Help students discover, secure, and manage student accommodation with confidence.

### Journey Steps
1. Sign up and create a profile.
2. Browse available listings and filter by budget, location, and amenities.
3. Save or shortlist preferred properties.
4. Book a viewing or submit an enquiry.
5. Receive confirmation and onboarding guidance.
6. Access the student hub for support, maintenance, communications, and events.
7. Track payments, attendance, compliance documents, and loyalty rewards.

### Key Experience
- Easy search and booking flow
- Personalized student support
- Centralized student hub for daily life and housing operations

---

## 2. Property Owner Journey

### Goal
Enable owners to manage properties, attract tenants, and monitor occupancy and revenue.

### Journey Steps
1. Register as a property owner.
2. Add a new listing with property details, pricing, and availability.
3. Review enquiries and bookings from students.
4. Approve or manage tenant requests and occupancy.
5. Monitor maintenance, invoices, and tenant communications.
6. Use the dashboard to view occupancy, revenue, and property health.
7. Create events, updates, and engagement activities for residents.

### Key Experience
- Simple property setup and management
- Visibility into performance and operations
- Better communication with students and agents

---

## 3. Agent Journey

### Goal
Support the property sales and rental process by connecting students with suitable listings.

### Journey Steps
1. Sign up and verify agent profile.
2. View available listings and manage enquiries.
3. Help students shortlist properties and arrange viewings.
4. Track listing engagement and lead progress.
5. Coordinate with owners and students on bookings and follow-ups.
6. Monitor dashboard metrics such as occupancy, enquiries, and pending actions.

### Key Experience
- Organized lead and listing management
- Strong coordination with owners and students
- Clear visibility into pipeline performance

---

## 4. University Journey

### Goal
Provide institutions with oversight of student accommodation and engagement workflows.

### Journey Steps
1. Register and access the institutional dashboard.
2. Review student accommodation activity and support needs.
3. Coordinate events, communications, and campus-related engagement.
4. Monitor compliance, documentation, and student welfare progress.
5. Use reporting and dashboard insights to support planning.

### Key Experience
- Improved student support visibility
- Better coordination with housing partners
- Stronger student engagement and welfare oversight

---

## 5. Admin Journey

### Goal
Operate and oversee the full platform ecosystem.

### Journey Steps
1. Sign in as an administrator.
2. Monitor platform activity, users, bookings, and support requests.
3. Review system-wide metrics and operational health.
4. Manage escalations, communications, and compliance items.
5. Ensure smooth performance across students, owners, agents, and universities.

### Key Experience
- Full operational oversight
- Better control of platform health and growth
- Centralized management of platform activity

---

## Summary

Each role has a distinct journey, but all of them connect around the same core platform goals:
- help users discover housing opportunities
- make communication and support easier
- improve operational visibility
- create a more complete student living experience
